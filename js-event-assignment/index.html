About Kimzie's

Welcome to Kimzie's — your cozy neighborhood bakery where love meets flour! We’re a passionate, family-owned bakery dedicated to crafting handmade pastries, breads, and sweets that warm your heart.

From buttery croissants to fluffy cupcakes, every bite is made fresh daily with premium ingredients and a sprinkle of care. Whether you’re stopping by for your morning treat or ordering for a special celebration, we’ve got something delightful waiting for you.

At Kimzie's, it’s not just about baking — it’s about creating moments that make life sweeter.

“Baked with love, served with joy.”
